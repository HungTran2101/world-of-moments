# Dear World

Dear World is a public map of anonymous thoughts tied to places. A thought can be a memory of a street, a feeling carried far from home, or a few words someone wanted to leave there. Place and weather give the words context. The map is the starting point for discovery.

## Exploring the map

Visitors can explore without signing in. Selecting a cluster brings the map closer. Selecting an individual pin opens its thought and weather description. An open thought has a shareable link. Zooming out reveals a satellite globe and star field, and zooming back in returns to the street map.

## Leaving a thought

1. Select a place on the map to place the writing pin.
2. Write a description in the sidebar. Sign in and verify an email address to submit.
3. Submit the thought. A bubble moves toward the selected place while the map settles on the pin.

The sidebar saves draft text in this browser after a short pause in typing and restores it when the account opens the form. A failed submission preserves the draft and pin for another attempt. Reloading the page requires placing the pin again. A successful submission clears that draft. Once saved, the words and coordinates are final. Authors can revisit or delete their own thoughts from private history in the toolbar. See [client accounts and ownership](client-auth.md) for visibility rules.

## Experience principles

Public thoughts omit names and exact timestamps. Likes, comments, followers, feeds and popularity rankings fall outside the product. Motion should help visitors follow a change of place or focus. New arrivals should never interrupt reading with sound or pop-ups.

The interface supports Vietnamese and English, with Vietnamese as the default. Visitors can choose the language and map lighting from the toolbar. Visual identity and artwork are described in [branding](branding.md).
